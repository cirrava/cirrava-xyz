Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Cirrava has no accounts, no sign-up, no tracking cookies and no analytics. This page explains the little data that is still processed when you use cirrava.xyz, and by whom.

1. What our server records

Like almost every website, our web server (nginx) keeps an access log of each request: your IP address, the date and time, the page or file requested, the response, the referring page and your browser's user-agent string. We use these logs only to keep the site running and secure, for example to stop abuse. They are deleted automatically after 14 days.

We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and we do not sell, rent or share data for advertising.

2. Your wallet and the blockchain

When you connect a wallet, the app reads your wallet address from your wallet in your browser in order to show your balances and positions. We do not store it on our server.

Anything you do on Robinhood Chain (deposits, loans, repayments, transfers) is recorded on a public blockchain. Anyone can see it, forever, and it can sometimes be linked to you. Neither we nor anyone else can delete it.

3. The RPC proxy

To read the blockchain, the app sends requests to cirrava.xyz/rpc, which forwards them to Robinhood Chain's public RPC. These requests can contain wallet addresses you are looking up. The proxy does not pass your IP address on to Robinhood Chain and does not log the contents of requests; the request itself appears in the server log described above. Transactions you sign are sent by your own wallet through the network it is configured to use, not by us.

4. Data kept in your browser

The site stores one value in your browser's local storage: your choice of light or dark theme (cirrava-theme). It never leaves your device. We use no cookies.

5. Third-party services

Loading the site contacts a few other services, which receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for typefaces;
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) for open-source JavaScript libraries;
  • your wallet software and the network it connects to, when you connect or sign.

Links to Blockscout, GitHub and X only contact those services if you follow them.

6. Security

The site is served over HTTPS only, with strict security headers. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we keep as little as possible so there is little to lose.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. Because we only hold short-lived server logs that are not linked to names or wallets, there is usually nothing we can identify as yours; we will still answer any request. Contact us as described below.

8. Children

Cirrava is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If what we process changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. The full history is public.

10. Contact

Send a direct message to @Cirrava on X.
