    Cirrava

    A lending market for Stock Tokens. Borrow against equity exposure without selling it; lend the stablecoin that borrowing is denominated in.

    Introduction

    Cirrava lets you borrow against Stock Tokens without selling them, and lets lenders earn yield on the stablecoin the borrowing is denominated in. It is live on Robinhood Chain mainnet (chain ID 4663) since 21 November 2026.

    Hold NVDA as a Stock Token and need cash without closing the position? Deposit it as collateral and borrow USDG. Hold USDG and want yield? Deposit it into the Earn vault, which lends to every market and earns what borrowers pay. Contracts hold the assets; nobody else does.

    Why stocks are different from ETH

    A Stock Token tracks an instrument that stops trading overnight, at weekends and on holidays, and its price feed stops with it. Cirrava treats a closed market as an expected state, not an oracle failure: the last price stands, borrowing and liquidations pause, and every risk-reducing action stays open.

    ActionWhat it does
    EarnDeposit USDG into the Earn vault and receive cvUSDG shares. The vault is the only lender to the markets.
    BorrowDeposit NVDA, SPY, TSLA, AAPL, MSFT, QQQ, GOOGL or AMZN (Robinhood Stock Tokens) and borrow USDG against it. A 0.1% fee is taken from each borrow.
    RepayIn USDG, from your wallet, at any time.

    How it works

    Each market is an isolated pair: one Stock Token as collateral, USDG to borrow. A position in NVDA/USDG is priced and liquidated on NVDA alone. Lenders share one Earn vault, and a cap per market (currently 10,000 USDG) bounds how much of the vault any one market can use.

    Borrowing

    Deposit collateral, borrow USDG up to the market's LTV, pay interest continuously, repay in USDG. Positions are recorded per wallet inside each market contract.

    Interest

    A kinked curve: 2% a year at zero utilization, rising to 10% at 80% utilization, then steeply to 110% at full utilization to pull liquidity back. Lenders earn what borrowers pay, minus a 10% reserve factor.

    Health and liquidation

    Two thresholds, with a deliberate gap: the LTV caps what you may borrow, and the liquidation threshold is where a position becomes liquidatable.

    borrowed value > collateral value × liquidation threshold

    A liquidator may repay up to half of an unhealthy position's debt (all of it below $100) and receives collateral worth the repaid amount plus a bonus: 5% in the regular markets, 7.5% in the 24/7 markets.

    Market hours

    The on-chain calendar follows the NYSE regular session, 09:30–16:00 New York time, Monday to Friday. US daylight-saving time is computed in the contract; NYSE holidays and early closes for 2026–2027 are stored on-chain.

    Everything that reduces risk stays available; everything that takes a new position on a frozen price does not. After the open, a price published before the open still counts as closed until Chainlink publishes a fresh in-session price.

    24/7 markets

    NVDA, TSLA and GOOGL also have a 24/7 market, priced by Chainlink's 24/7 feeds for those Stock Tokens. These markets have no exchange calendar: borrowing and liquidations are open whenever the price updated within the last 3 hours, weekends included, and pause when the feed goes quiet. Off-hours prices are thinner and can jump, so these markets have lower LTVs, a 7.5% liquidation bonus and a smaller cap. The regular markets for the same tokens keep following NYSE hours.

    Expect a 24/7 market to pause often. Chainlink's 24/7 feeds publish on a 0.5% price move, so a quiet market or a stopped feed both look like silence. Over 14 days in November 2026 the TSLA and GOOGL 24/7 feeds went more than 3 hours without an update about 30% of the time, and NVDA about 16%, with gaps of up to 18.7 hours at night and over weekends. During those gaps the market shows Waiting for price: borrowing and liquidations pause, repaying and adding collateral stay open.

    Splits and corporate actions

    While Robinhood processes a corporate action on a Stock Token (a split, reverse split or dividend reinvested through the token's multiplier) the token reports oraclePaused(), and Robinhood says the price must not be trusted until it clears. Every Cirrava price oracle reads that flag, and the token's own paused(): while either is set, borrowing, liquidations and withdrawing collateral against debt pause for that token, exactly as when the market is closed. Repaying and adding collateral stay open.

    There is no override: if Robinhood kept the flag up, borrowing and liquidations for that token would stay paused until it clears, and the owner cannot switch the check off.

    Risks

    Read this before you deposit or borrow. Cirrava's contracts have not been audited. Deposits and loans use real assets and can be lost.

    Admin key

    One wallet currently owns every contract. It can change risk parameters, fees and the interest-rate model, pause a market, add markets to the Earn vault, set their caps, move vault liquidity between markets and withdraw protocol reserves. Lowering a liquidation threshold can make positions liquidatable, and adding a market lets that wallet route vault deposits to it, so a lost or stolen admin key puts deposits at risk. Moving ownership to a multisig is on the roadmap.

    No upgrades

    The contracts are not upgradeable proxies: the code at each address is fixed. A fix would mean deploying new contracts.

    Oracle dependencies

    Positions are priced from Chainlink's feeds on Robinhood Chain. A paused, stale or wrong feed stops or misprices borrowing and liquidations. Prices are also unavailable while Robinhood processes a corporate action on a Stock Token; if that lasted, liquidations for that token would stay paused and nothing on Cirrava's side can let them through, so an unhealthy position could not be closed until Robinhood clears it.

    Security review

    HEY Research Lab reviewed the contracts for free in November 2026 and reported one Low-severity issue: the price oracles did not read the Stock Token's oraclePaused() flag during corporate actions. It was fixed in v2 (see Splits and corporate actions) before any deposits, and HEY's retest of the deployed v2 contracts confirmed the fix with no new findings at Low or above. A security review is not an audit and is no guarantee that the code is safe.

    Weekend gaps

    Prices can move sharply between Friday's close and Monday's open; a position can open the week already liquidatable.

    Liquidity

    Vault withdrawals are limited to USDG that is not lent out. If markets are fully borrowed, a withdrawal waits until borrowers repay.

    Cirrava v2: a security fix and 24/7 markets

    27 November 2026

    HEY Research Lab reviewed Cirrava's contracts for free and reported one Low-severity issue, privately and before any deposits. Robinhood marks a Stock Token with oraclePaused() while it processes a split or another corporate action, and says the price must not be trusted until it clears. Our price oracles did not read that flag. In the rare case a feed published a wrongly scaled price in that window, healthy positions could have been liquidated cheaply, or a market's cash borrowed against inflated collateral.

    The oracles now read the flag, and the token's own paused(): while either is set, borrowing, liquidations and withdrawing collateral against debt pause for that token, as they do when the market is closed. Repaying and adding collateral stay open. Because the contracts are not upgradeable, Cirrava was redeployed as v2 with a new Earn vault, oracles and markets, and every v1 market was paused. No funds were ever at risk: the vault was empty. Thanks to HEY Research Lab. A security review is not an audit, and the contracts remain unaudited.

    v2 also adds 24/7 markets for NVDA, TSLA and GOOGL, priced by Chainlink's 24/7 feeds. They lend whenever the price updated in the last 3 hours, weekends included, with tighter limits than the regular markets:

    What happens when the market closes

    21 November 2026

    A Stock Token follows a share that only trades 09:30–16:00 New York time, Monday to Friday. When the exchange shuts, its price feed stops moving. Cirrava treats that as a normal state rather than an error.

    While the market is closed, anything that would take a new position on a frozen price waits: borrowing, liquidations and withdrawing collateral from a position that has debt. Anything that reduces risk keeps working: repaying, adding collateral, withdrawing collateral with no debt, and depositing into or withdrawing from the Earn vault.

    At the open, a price published before 09:30 still counts as closed until Chainlink publishes a fresh one during the session, so nobody borrows on Friday's close. In Indonesia, the US open is 21:30 WIB from November to March and 20:30 WIB from March to November. Weekends, NYSE holidays and early closes are all handled on-chain; the app's Status page shows the live state.

    Health factor, LTV and liquidation

    21 November 2026

    Two numbers set the limits of a position. The LTV is the most you can borrow when you open or increase a loan. The liquidation threshold is the level at which the position can be liquidated. The gap between them is your buffer.

    Worked example on NVDA/USDG, with NVDA at $130:

    Value
    Collateral: 10 NVDA$1,300
    Most you can borrow (60%)$780 USDG
    Liquidatable once debt exceeds (65%)$845
    If you borrow the full $780, liquidation price$120 per NVDA

    The health factor is collateral value × liquidation threshold ÷ debt. Above 1 is safe; below 1 the position can be liquidated. Borrowing $500 against the 10 NVDA above gives 1,300 × 0.65 ÷ 500 = 1.69.

    A liquidator repays up to half of an unhealthy position's debt (all of it if the debt is under $100) and receives collateral worth that amount plus a 5% bonus. Interest accrues every second, so a position drifts toward liquidation slowly even if the price does not move. Repaying or adding collateral raises the health factor at any time, including while the market is closed.
