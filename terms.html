1. What Cirrava is

Cirrava is a set of smart contracts on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) that let people lend USDG through an Earn vault and borrow USDG against Robinhood Stock Tokens, together with a website and app that read those contracts and help you send transactions to them.

Cirrava is non-custodial. The team never holds your assets or your keys. Every deposit, loan, repayment and liquidation is carried out by the contracts when you, or someone else, sends a transaction. Transactions on a blockchain are final: nobody, including the team, can reverse one.

The source code is public on GitHub and every contract address is listed in the docs.

5. Admin powers

Every Cirrava contract is owned by a 48-hour timelock (0xF71D…1D06), and only the Cirrava Safe multisig (0xe7FD…8c2e, 2 of 3 signers) can propose to it. Through it the owner can change risk parameters, fees and the interest-rate model, add markets to the Earn vault, set caps, move vault liquidity between markets and withdraw protocol reserves; each change waits 48 hours after it is scheduled. The Safe can pause markets at once in an emergency. The contracts are not upgradeable. The risks section of the docs explains what these powers mean for you.

6. Fees

Each borrow pays a 0.1% fee, and 10% of the interest borrowers pay goes to the protocol reserve (the reserve factor). Both go to the treasury address set in the contracts. You also pay Robinhood Chain network fees for every transaction. Fees are enforced by the contracts and may change through the admin powers above.
